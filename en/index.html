In addition to the website

Need anything else? We’re here for that too

The website is the most common starting point, but it’s not the only way we help local businesses. Here are a few examples of what we do in addition to building websites:

Loyalty points without a loyalty card

No more loyalty cards to stamp and lose: the customer scans a QR code and their loyalty scheme is stored on their phone, ready for every visit.

The customer saves your number straight away

A sticker in the shop window or at the till: customers simply hold their phone up to it and your contact details are instantly added to their address book, without having to download anything.

A photo corner in the shop

A small display designed to be photographed: customers take their own photos, tag you, and the publicity continues without you having to give it a second thought.
